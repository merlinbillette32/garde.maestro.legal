Maestro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Maestro is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when users connect third-party services such as TikTok, Meta, Google, or other supported platforms.

Information We Access

Maestro may access information authorized by the user through official platform APIs, including profile information, account statistics, content lists, and performance metrics.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide Maestro features requested by the user, such as analytics, account supervision, reporting, and workflow automation.

Data Sharing

Maestro does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where required to provide requested services, comply with applicable law, or protect users and the service.

User Control

Users may disconnect supported third-party accounts and revoke platform permissions at any time through the applicable provider.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the Maestro service operator.
